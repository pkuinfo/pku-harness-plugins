# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the PKU Harness
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text applies; this page highlights what matters most here.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and constructive; assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers: many contributors are students writing their first open-source code.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language and personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information. In this project that explicitly includes student IDs,
  names, campus account details, grades, card transactions and treehole content that could
  identify someone, including in screenshots, logs and test fixtures.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainers of the [pkuinfo](https://github.com/pkuinfo)
organization (a direct message on GitHub to any maintainer). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may respond with a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, following the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
